## 1. THE MATHEMATICS — each item with its estimator, its error bar, its kill

### 1.1 THE HILBERT METRIC AND THE CONTRACTION CERTIFICATE (ARM S core)

On the open simplex, for `p, q > 0`:

    d_H(p, q) = log( max_j (p_j/q_j) / min_j (p_j/q_j) )
              = max_j log(p_j/q_j) − min_j log(p_j/q_j)

An **oscillation of log-ratios**: `O(s)`, no `sqrt`, no `arccos`, **no
ill-conditioning near coincidence** — the direct answer to F-identity's
arccos-near-1 collapse.

Settling map `T(m) = Σ_p w_p(m)·a_p` normalised, `w` a positive kernel over pivot
readings `a_p`. **Exact form fixed by Foreman in Phase A.** `T` MUST be positive:
every entry of every `a_p > 0` (softmax rows guarantee it) and `w_p > 0`.

**BIRKHOFF [U — Foreman fetches BEFORE any load-bearing use].** A positive linear
map `L` with projective diameter `Δ(L) = sup_{p,q} d_H(Lp, Lq)` contracts `d_H`
with ratio `κ(L) = tanh(Δ(L)/4) < 1` whenever `Δ(L) < ∞`.

**ESTIMATORS, both required, both printed:**
* `κ̂_emp` = median over draw pairs of `d_H(Tp,Tq)/d_H(p,q)`, bootstrap CI B=10,000
* `Δ̂` = the diameter **AT THE EXTREME RAYS**, giving `κ̂_cert = tanh(Δ̂/4)`

**⚠ SECOND PRE-DATUM REPAIR — `Δ̂` WAS DEFINED AS A SAMPLED MAXIMUM AND THAT IS
NOT A CERTIFICATE. [RUN, this session, `tests/loop/test_settle.py`]** The first
draft read *"`Δ̂` = max over **sampled pairs**"*. **The supremum is attained at the
extreme rays of the cone, not in its interior** — for a positive matrix `A`,
`A e_i` is exactly column `i`, so the diameter is the largest `d_H` between
COLUMNS, and uniform interior draws never approach it:

    seed 0   columns 2.557159   uniform sampling 1.358710   53.1% of truth
    seed 1   columns 2.643721   uniform sampling 1.281507   48.5% of truth
    seed 2   columns 3.411853   uniform sampling 1.329837   39.0% of truth

    kappa from truth     0.564416 / 0.578982 / 0.692614
    kappa from sampling  0.327189 / 0.309848 / 0.320729

**A sampled `Δ̂` is a LOWER bound on the diameter presented as an UPPER bound on
`κ` — it errs OPTIMISTIC, halving the apparent contraction ratio.** That is
round 5's defect class exactly: a gate measuring something adjacent to its
pre-registration, erring flattering. **And it chains** — `1/(1−κ)` is 1.2's
Neumann conditioning, so an optimistic `κ` under-budgets `N` and the round would
have discovered it at Phase D. Binding:

  * **`T` linear in `m` ⇒ take the columns.** Exact, no sampling, no CI needed:
    `scale/settle.py::column_diameter`.
  * **`T` NOT linear ⇒ the column trick does not apply, and Birkhoff's hypotheses
    may not be met either** (it is stated for positive **linear** maps, and `T`'s
    `w_p(m)` depends on `m`). Foreman must answer this plainly. Meanwhile sample
    with draws concentrated toward the corners — `rand(n)**40 + 1e-12` recovers
    the algebraic diameter to `rel=1e-6` where uniform reaches under half.
  * **Report BOTH** the extreme-ray and interior-sampled `Δ̂`, so the gap stays
    visible instead of being a choice made on the reader's behalf.

**CONSISTENCY GATE:** `κ̂_emp ≤ κ̂_cert`, with a must-fire in which a doctored
non-positive `T` violates it.

**CONVERGENCE LAW:** `d_H(m_t,m*) ≤ κ^t·d_H(m_0,m*)`, so
`t* = ⌈log(d_H(m_0,m*)/tol)/log(1/κ)⌉`. Round 5 measured **28–53 steps**; Chase
back-fits `κ_fit = exp(−1/τ)` from that journal as a free cross-check on `κ̂_emp`.

### 1.2 THE IMPLICIT GRADIENT (ARM S training)

Fixed point `m* = T(m*, φ)`. IFT [V]:
`∂m*/∂φ = (I − ∂T/∂m|_{m*})^{-1} · ∂T/∂φ|_{m*}`, with
`‖(I−J)^{-1}‖ ≤ 1/(1−κ)` — **the conditioning IS the contraction certificate.**

Neumann truncation `(I−J)^{-1} ≈ Σ_{i<N} J^i`, error `≤ κ^N/(1−κ)` **[U bound —

exactly where the theory says it must.

### 1.3 THE FOUR-POINT WALSH PROBE (ARM P core)

`f(S)` = a scalar readout of `A(·| mask S)`, `S ⊆ {c,j}`. Degree-2 interaction:

    I(c,j) = f(∅) − f({c}) − f({j}) + f({c,j})

`I = 4·f̂({c,j})` exactly when higher coefficients vanish on the restriction; in
general it carries degree-≥3 leakage, bounded empirically by the 8-point triple
mask `L₃` with **`E|L₃|/E|I| > 0.5` voiding degree-2 claims at that geometry**.

**DEGREE AUDIT (Foreman).** Every proposed statistic is expanded on the 4-point
lattice; **an affine function of `{f(∅), f({c})}` alone is degree ≤ 1 and is
STRUCK unbuilt.** This is F-identity mechanised as algebra rather than judgment.

**VARIANCE.** `Var(Î) ≈ 4σ²_f/n`; **`n` from Chase's Phase-B power analysis at
0.9, no round numbers.**

**THE F-IDENTITY TRAP, CLOSED.** With `|S| = 2` the renormalised row has **two**
degrees of freedom `(p_c, p_j)`, so no scalar identity can collapse the probe.
**Cameron states this as a lemma with a numerical 2-dof Jacobian rank check per
geometry** — the lemma is not believed until the rank is measured.
