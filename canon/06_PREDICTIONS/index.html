    I. The rule and the column#

    06.1 — The scoring rule, frozen#

    Statement. Every prediction row of the canon is scored with exactly one token of \(\{\mathrm{HOLDS},\mathrm{COUNTER},\mathrm{SPLIT},\mathrm{VOID}\}\) by the adjudicator that reads only journals (S-63): HOLDS when the deciding number lies in the prediction's region; COUNTER when it lies in the counter's; SPLIT when it lies in any region the verse declares outside both halves — the band between them, and, where a verse's prediction is a two-sided range, the region beyond the range's far end — scored wrong with the sign the verse prints for that region (the sign of the optimistic half where the verse names none). The generalisation from "the band between" is made here because a two-sided prediction has two regions outside it and only one of them lies between the halves: 06.19's upper break \(\Delta_{A1}>0.85\) is reachable on the bed as registered and, under the narrower reading, carried no token at all, which is the condition 06.36's Kill declares refuting. The token set stays at four; VOID when the bed was not admitted or a prerequisite instrument failed its own plants, unscored and never a miss. Rows are appended, never edited; a corrected reading is a new row whose supersedes field names the old row (L-G2). The one-sided sign test \(p_k=\Pr[X\ge k]\), \(X\sim\mathrm{Bin}(8,\tfrac12)\), runs on the pooled column after eight scored rows and on the author and leap sub-columns separately: \(p_8=1/256=0.003906\), \(p_7=9/256=0.035156\), \(p_6=37/256=0.144531\), \(p_5=93/256=0.363281\) (DERIVED, binomial tail). The \(0.75\) rule is a threshold, not a test: a column at or above \(6\) of \(8\) optimistic makes the counter the reported estimate for every unscored row, although \(p_6=0.1445\) does not clear \(\alpha=0.05\); both numbers are printed beside the column so the rule cannot be read as a significance claim.
