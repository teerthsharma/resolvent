Opened at **it.7** by JUPITER, on the author's instruction — *"if something fails, all
that adds to the leap."* The contract's it.35 requires JUPITER + MARS to grade every
F1/F2/F3 failure **LEAPABLE** (naming the **FIELD** likely to hold the missing theorem —
never the theorem) or **TERMINAL** (a bound; no theorem removes a bound).

**A failure graded when it happens is worth more than the same failure reconstructed nine
iterations later.** Rows are appended when the failure is produced, not at it.35.

**Grading rule used here.** A row is TERMINAL when the gap is a *bound* — an inequality
that holds for every instrument in the class, so no theorem can remove it. A row is
LEAPABLE when the gap is a *missing statement or a missing measurement* — something a
theorem or an instrument change could supply.
