## 6. Phase 5 — the cost law and the memory ceiling (the `s`-sweep is the precondition for any cost law)

### 5.0 The residency model that sets `n` per `s` (DERIVED; `[ASSUMED]` band on `C_OPERATOR`)

Bytes `≈ 1.256 × [C_OP · n·s²·4 + 2·n·s·d·4 + 17.874·n·s·d·4]`, with `1.256` the worst reserved/allocated ratio over resident shapes (`READ COSTS.md` §3.2), `C_RESIDUAL = 17.874` (R² `0.996373`, `READ COSTS.md:89`), `C_OP ∈ [3.823, 5.823]` — the signed arm's `3.823` (R² `0.996373`, `:90`) plus `1…2` retained tensors for `M` and `M̄` (`sec_cost.md` §4.x.7, **[ASSUMED]**; M2.3's memory census replaces it), budget `0.90 × 7.996 = 7.20 GiB`. Largest power-of-two `n` that fits (`RUN`, the one-liner of §0.3):

| `s` | `n` at `C_OP = 3.823` | `n` at `C_OP = 5.823` (carried) | check |
|---|---|---|---|
| 64 | 32768 (`5.52 GiB`) | 32768 (`6.78 GiB`) | softmax measured resident at 32768, `4.908 GiB` (`READ COSTS.md:112`) |
| 256 | 4096 (`6.36`) | **2048** (`4.44`) | — |
| 1024 | 256 (`5.19`) | **128** (`3.85`) | — |
