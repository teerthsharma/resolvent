# D1 — The negative result

D1 is named twice in `LOOP_PROMPT.md` (clause 10, and the completion section) and
was defined only in `LOOP_PROMPT_ROUND4_ARCHIVE.md:125`. No standalone document
existed until now; `PROGNOSIS.md` carries D1 in substance under another name for
rounds 1–4, with no acceptance criteria and no round-5 chapter. This file supplies
the criteria and the chapter.

## What D1 is, and when it is complete

D1 is the deliverable that ships when the round's positive claim does not. It is
complete when all five hold:

1. Every kill that fired is named with **the number that fired it and that
   number's interval**, not a point estimate.
2. Every instrument defect found during the round is recorded, **including which
   were caught by the project's own output and which were caught by someone
   else**.
3. Every claim that was withdrawn is recorded **with what replaced it**.
4. What survives is stated separately from what died, and **neither is padded**.
5. Limits are collected **once**, at the end.

## Round 5 — CEQ v7, the two-spheres round

**Verdict: the round's central kill fired. No positive claim survives.**

The round proposed Displacement-Equilibrium Attention: measure a token's
consequence as an **angle** on the unit sphere, via the square-root map
`φ(p) = √p` that carries the simplex isometrically onto the positive orthant,
with the Fisher–Rao metric picked out uniquely by Čencov's theorem. The stated
payoff was that displacement would be **scale-free by radius rather than by
tuning**.

### What died, by which number

**K1, the dual-slope kill — FIRED.** Its displacement clause reads, over 400
draws per cell at six pivot counts, bucketed, with a determinism replay matching
each bucket:

| statistic | slope | 95% CI | vs the −0.30 line | vs K1's bar ≥ −0.10 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| as computed | **−0.4137** | [−0.4579, −0.3704] | excludes, CI below | not met |
| live rows only | **STRUCK — unverified** | **STRUCK — unverified** | — | — |

The contract's pre-registered trigger is *"if `D_FR` slope < −0.3, displacement
dies with flip and the answer was 'no leap.'"* Both intervals lie entirely below
that line, so the trigger fires; both also fail K1's own bar by a wide margin.
Per `LOOP_PROMPT.md:162`, ARM A has not survived K1, so **ARM B was never
authorized and was never built**.

K1's sign-flip clause is **alive but unresolved**. On the unsigned arm it was zero
by theorem — the influence Jacobian `I + A + A²` is entrywise non-negative, so the
gradient sign cannot change, and a control that cannot be nonzero is not a
control. On the signed arm it reads 8 events in 2400 draws, 0.003333 with an exact
interval [0.001440, 0.006557]. Its slope, −0.6960 with a CI of [−1.0000, +0.0000],
straddles the −0.4 bar, and that interval is **count discreteness rather than an
interval**: the unsigned arm's exact interval [0, 0.010195] *contains* the signed
arm's pooled rate, so at these sample sizes the two arms are not separated even
though one is zero by theorem and the other is not. Resolving it requires the
contract's own 20,000 draws per cell, about eight hours at the measured per-draw
cost.

**K3, the geometry clause — passes as written, and cannot mean what it was
written to mean.** Masking one token and renormalising leaves the row a single
degree of freedom, so with `p = A^c[i,c]`:

    A^0[i,j] = A^c[i,j] / (1 − p)    BC_i = √(1 − p)    TV_i = p

    θ_i = arcsin(√(TV_i))     exactly, row by row

Measured three times independently: float32 residual **8.457280e-04**, float64
**6.828570e-08**, and a total-variation residual of **2.980e-07** at the float32
floor for a 1024-term sum. The four-order collapse from single to double precision
is the signature of an exact identity read through an ill-conditioned `arccos`
near argument 1.

The angle is therefore a fixed strictly-increasing function of total variation, so
K3 compares **two aggregations of one number**. It can only ever win by Jensen's
inequality on a concave map, and beating raw total variation is evidence of a
square root rather than of a sphere. Against a held-out concave control `TV^p`,
chosen on one half of the draws and scored on the other:

| pivots | θ − raw TV | θ − held-out `TV^p` |
|---|---|---|
| 8 | +0.0157 [+0.0057, +0.0241] | **−0.0643 [−0.0981, −0.0159]** |
| 32 | +0.0282 [+0.0100, +0.0405] | −0.0155 [−0.1587, +0.1924] |
| 128 | +0.0387 [+0.0215, +0.0577] | −0.0500 [−0.1670, +0.1133] |

The angle beats raw total variation at every pivot count with intervals excluding
zero, and **loses at the smallest count** to a plain power of total variation that
carries no metric, no invariance theorem and no inverse cosine.

**The sphere's curvature is measured as a liability.** The chord and `sin θ = √TV`
are the tangent-plane readings, and both beat the geodesic on K3's own criterion:
the chord by 0.86%, 0.65% and 1.25%, and `√TV` by 3.97%, 2.87% and 5.70%. The one
feature distinguishing the sphere from the flat simplex costs standardized effect
at every pivot count measured.

**The aggregator advantage — real as a comparison, largely confounded as a
result.** Taking the maximum row angle rather than the mean gains
**+1.1347 [+0.7833, +1.5877]**, +0.9355 and +0.4959, intervals excluding zero,
against a K3 quarrel of +0.0141 to +0.0298. Two things then follow. First, by the
identity, `max_i θ_i = arcsin(√(max_i A^c[i,c]))`, so the statistic is a monotone
read of **the largest attention weight any row places on the masked token**, with
an area-under-curve gap of about 1e-03 against that raw quantity — the sphere
contributes nothing to the advantage. Second, the pivot selector ranks by
`key.norm(dim=-1)` (`scale/pivot_probe.py:88`) and the causal arm is exactly its
top-k, so the contrast is confounded by construction. Against a key-norm-matched
filler drawn from ranks k+1..2k:

| pivots | causal vs tail | causal vs matched band | retained |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 2.2994 [1.8663, 2.8623] | 0.0677 [0.0040, 0.2978] | **2.9%** |
| 32 | 1.6804 [1.3574, 2.0972] | 0.2241 [0.0274, 0.4494] | 13.3% |
| 128 | 1.4204 [1.1252, 1.7744] | 0.8366 [0.6020, 1.1082] | 58.9% |

The mean-based comparison retained 35% under the same control. The aggregator
retains 2.9%. **It is more confounded by the selector than the statistic it was
meant to improve on, not less.** A larger effect on a confounded contrast is a
larger confounded effect.

**The additive-basis arm's third birth gate — FAILED, and the failure is real.**
On-schedule sign-flip rate 17/384 against off-schedule 0/384 with disjoint
intervals. An exact zero over a full cell is the signature that voided K1's flip
half, so the off-schedule cell was audited for vacuity: 109 of 384 draws have
gradient branches that differ, with a largest separation of 1.505102e-02, about
3.7% of the on-schedule maximum and thirteen orders above double-precision
rounding at that scale. Influence exists off the schedule and the sign holds
through all of it, so the gate fails on a measurement rather than on a theorem.

**B1, the first proposed upgrade — not a rename, and ill-posed as written.** Its
score does not collapse onto the incumbent: rank correlation between candidate
key-norm and candidate shadow mass reads −0.0253, with top-k overlap below chance
at every pivot count, reproduced twice. But the shadow is defined **relative to a
chosen masked token**, while the selector it would replace is documented to use
*"ONLY CONTENT — never `c`, never `i`, `j`."* Recomputing the shadow at a
different masked position on the same draw retains 5.4% of the top-8 selection,
19.6% of the top-32 and 63.2% of the top-128. A criterion that keeps a twentieth
of its picks when something it may not see changes is not a selector. This is a
defect in the specification sentence, found before any of it was built.

### What survives

**The equilibrium clause survives its own kill.** X6 pre-registered that if one
pass already settles, the word "equilibrium" is cut. Starting the Karcher
iteration at the softmax reading itself, the residual at that starting point reads
0.599101, 0.388587 and 0.321843, with intervals nowhere near zero, and every draw
converges to 1e-8 in 28 to 53 steps. The glance is not the fixed point.

**A contradiction inside the contract was repaired by measurement.** The document
defined equilibrium twice — once as the torque vanishing, once as the settled
reading being the Karcher mean — and those are different points. Written out for
this iteration, the torque vanishes exactly when the reading is parallel to the
plain Euclidean average of the pivots, and that average is a closed form: it reads
a torque between 2.45e-16 and 5.18e-15, machine zero, **in one pass with no
iteration**. So the torque branch triggers X6's own kill. The contract now names
the Karcher residual as the certificate and reclassifies the torque as a
displacement statistic.

**Two facts about softmax attention, independent of the frame.** Peak attention on
a selected token reaches 0.884602 against 0.160338 on an unselected one. And under
strictly-causal masking, shadow mass is exactly zero for 68.04% of candidates —
row `p` moves only if it attended to the masked token, which requires `p > c`.

### Limits

Every figure above is at s=1024, d=16, threads pinned to 2 in the measuring file,
on CPU, with several agents sharing the machine — so no wall-clock timing appears
anywhere in this chapter and none should be inferred. Draw counts are declared per
table and are below the contract's 20,000 in every case; the sign-flip clause is
unresolved for exactly that reason. The key-norm-matched filler matches by **rank,
not by value** — band-to-causal key-norm runs 0.9120, 0.8900, 0.8347 — so the
retained fractions are an upper bound on what survives the confound, not a lower
one. The three-point slope at 120 draws that opened the round cannot be given an
interval at all, because the journal stores cell means and no per-draw values.
What peak attention tracks, once the key-norm confound is removed, is **not
established**.

The displacement slope that fired the round's central kill rests on a **journal
replay rather than a fresh derivation**: the driver reported every unit already
journalled and recomputed none of them. The replay matched bitwise, which is real
evidence the journal is intact, but nobody re-derived that slope from draws this
pass, and the figure should be read with that in mind.

The health inspection has now returned and its three strikes are applied above.
Two gaps in it remain open by its own account: the verified-facts pass was outside
its mandate and was not re-checked, and one claim about failures in a second

## Rounds 7–8 — the unposed question, and the reading that finally took it

**Verdict: no kill fired on settling, because at three of four rungs nothing beat
predict-the-mean and at the fourth the interval cannot decide at N = 5. The round's
product is that the deciding measurement finally exists, taken under a
pre-registration written before any of its numbers did — plus a registered corpus

### The unposed question

Seven rounds built toward one contrast — `settled` versus `twin`, iteration versus
no iteration at matched parameters — and the contrast had been measured exactly
once, on tasks where it could not matter. Every oracle registered before round 8
was a **closed-form function of its input**: a product of two entries
(`scale/negation_scope.py:68`) or a sum squared (`scale/negation_scope.py:127`), so
an arm that iterates had nothing to compute toward (READ, `LOOP_PROMPT.md:89-94`).
On those tasks the deciding contrast read `settled − twin = −0.002959`, exact 95 %
CI `[−0.042903, +0.031557]` over all 3125 paired resamples, covering zero (READ,
`CHECKLIST.md:1167`; `results/m3_quintuple.txt:51`). That number was correct and
uninformative — the right answer to a question no task had asked. `done7.md` scored
the product at **37 %** — engineering ≈ 80 %, scientific claim ≈ 5 % — and named
the reason in one sentence: *the deciding measurement had been taken ZERO times*
(READ, `done7.md:10,:45`; `STATE.md` scoreboard row).

Round 8 posed it. The new oracle is an absorbing-chain solve — `N = (I − Q)^{-1}`,
`B = NR`, the fixed point of `z ← Qz + R` — which is an equilibrium in the exact
sense the goal names and provably not a closed form of any bounded neighbourhood
(READ, `LOOP_PROMPT.md:81-94`). Everything below is measured on that round's ladder
instrument; what the instrument found is reported in the pre-registration's own
order.

### The reversal: softmax is the base case, not the opponent

The contract amended itself mid-round, on evidence: *"Softmax is not the opponent —
it is the base case, and the module must contain it"* (READ,
`LOOP_PROMPT.md:26-57`). Three independent results forced it: softmax is
Bayes-optimal on the single-location task shape (`arXiv:2410.01537`) and the
shallow rung of this project's own ladder **is** that shape; its single step is
already a converged modern-Hopfield update that *"converges with one update"*
(`arXiv:2008.02217`); iterating the normalisation is published and buys little
(Sinkformer, `arXiv:2110.11773`). The design consequence is exact: if softmax lies
inside the module's function class, the module is bounded below by it and every
equilibrium mechanism is a strict addition; what was actually built excludes it, so
the comparison measured the exclusion rather than the idea. Full derivations and
evidence classes in `MATHEMATICS.md:188-191` (CITED via `LOOP_PROMPT.md` amendment).

### Confound findings, compressed to what survives

Of STATE items 18–22 and their sequels, four things survive compression:

1. **The e-ladder comparison is confounded by construction, and the exclusion is a
   documented rationale nobody recognised as a capability ceiling.** Pivot selection
   excludes `s−1` then drops `0` (`scale/arm_s.py:107`; `scale/m3_quintuple.py:129`),
   so the max pivot is `s−2` and `tril(-1)` caps that row at `j ≤ s−3` — no pivot
   row can put `v[s−2]` on the value path while the t* = 1 label is `a[s−1]·b[s−2]`.
   Perturbing `s−2` moves softmax by **101.6983** and the pivot `av` by **3.263746**
   (READ, STATE item 18).
2. **Lifting the exclusion does not help — MOVE 1 is dead, pre-registered and
   cheap.** With the slot reserved, `twin_plus 0.938728` against a threshold of
   `0.871391`, **0.015610 worse than twin**; whatever costs the pivot cells
   `0.103453` at t* = 1, it is not pivot access (READ, STATE items 31–32). The
   inert version of this repair was caught before shipping: `exclude=(0,)` merely
   made `s−1` eligible in a top-k that selected it in 0 of 32 examples — a vacuous
   control nearly shipped as the cure for vacuous controls.
3. **The hop wall is depth.** `arXiv:2402.09268` Thm 4.2: `hop_k` needs
   `L = ⌊log₂ k⌋ + 2`; these arms are depth-1 (pivot cells read a two-hop
   neighbourhood), and t* = 8 needs ≈ 5. At t* = 8 the arm trains to `0.860972`,
   on its own two-hop ceiling `0.866025`, then evaluates `1.112208` — memorising
   noise past its budget (READ, STATE item 21). This is the mechanism row G was
   expected to fire on, and it fired.
4. **Settling adds variance and can reallocate, not add.** The settled cell iterates
   an `alpha` reparameterisation on an 8-simplex over `{alpha @ av}` — inside the
   twin's own family — which explains `0.002190` of mean gain carrying **3.874×**
   the twin's variance (`sd 0.064106` vs `0.016547`) (READ, STATE items 6, 20).
   And the pilot spread was **2.18× optimistic** — realised sd `0.109199` against a
   piloted `0.050146`, true five-seed half-width ≈ 0.0957, not 0.043955 — which is
   why the t* = 1 rung reads UNDERPOWERED rather than null (READ, STATE item 34).

### The curvature ledger entry

### What would falsify each surviving claim

1. **"Row G rungs credit nothing" (capacity reading).** Train arms at the depth the
   prescription demands (≈ ⌊log₂ t*⌋ + 2) at t* = 8/32: if cells fall below
   predict-the-mean and contrasts then separate, the capacity diagnosis stands; if
   deepened arms still sit at or above 1.0, the budget explanation is wrong and the
   shortfall is elsewhere (readout, objective, corpus).
2. **"t* = 1 is underpowered, not null."** The owed route decides it: 13 seeds at
   that rung. An interval excluding zero either direction kills the no-difference
   reading; a tighter interval still covering zero with |delta| < 0.027260 converts
   the rung to K-3E territory — settling retires, the twin ships.
3. **"The mixture is the gain" (twin − softmax +0.111396 [+0.100873, +0.121920],
   5/5).** Falsified by any matched-parameter rerun whose paired interval covers
   zero, or by `argmax` recovering (if argmax − softmax stops being negative, the
   gain was never the mixture).
4. **"Iterating buys little at shallow depth" (the reversal's evidence half).**
   g(t*) growing monotonically with disjoint intervals on the absorbing-chain
   corpus once registered — settling buying something softmax cannot reach —
   falsifies the "buys little" half while confirming the containment design.
5. **"E4′ admissible at n = 1024."** Any local feature class leaking below FAIL_BAR
   at radius ≤ 5 on fresh draws, or a planted control that stops firing, strikes the
   admissibility.
6. **"CUDA parity at tolerance."** Any unit whose CUDA−CPU delta exceeds
   atol 2e-4 / rtol 1e-4, or two CUDA runs of the same unit disagreeing, breaks the
   lane's determinism claim.
7. **"Weights artifact bit-exact."** Any recomputed NRMSE from a shipped safetensors
   diverging from its MANIFEST value beyond 1e-6, or a changed sha256 for a source
   checkpoint.

### Limits
