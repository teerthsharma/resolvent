## 6. THE `sign(a)` PROBE, AND THE CONTROL THAT NEARLY VOIDS IT

The registered diagnostic is refused. Re-measured on this run's own corpus:
**`SST` of `log|a|` on the live band = `0.000000e+00`.** `R²` on a target with
zero variance is `0/0`. The contract's kill condition cannot fire and cannot
fail to fire (M-18), so it is not run as a diagnostic here — only printed as the
control it has become.

The corrected diagnostic is a linear probe from the arm's own gate output
`(g_j, s_j)` at the live positions to `sign(a_j)`, **fit on the train batch and
scored on the eval batch**.

| probe | mean over 8 seeds | 95% CI |
|---|---|---|
| `sign(a)` accuracy `p`, trained | **0.917953** | [0.790669, 1.045238] |
| `sign(a)` accuracy `p₀`, **0 steps, same seeds** | **0.943741** | [0.893282, 0.994200] |
| `c = 2p − 1` | **0.835907** | — |
| gate `R²`, `â = exp(g)` vs `a`, trained | **0.699299** | [0.338436, 1.060162] |
| gate `R²₀`, **0 steps, same seeds** | **0.371839** | [0.195975, 0.547703] |

> **`p` reads WORSE trained than untrained: the trained gain is `−0.025787`.**

**Why, and it is the corpus again.** `make_batch` fills the noise channels at
`randn * 0.1` while `CH_DRIVE` carries `a` at modulus exactly `1`
(`scale/negation_scope.py:428`, and `:245`/`:432` put it in the input at all).
Any random linear readout of `x` is therefore dominated by the drive channel, and
an **untrained** scalar gate head already recovers `sign(a)` at `0.9437` — seed
3's untrained gate reads `p₀ = 1.0000` exactly. The probe is near-saturated
before the first gradient step, so it has almost no room to record a trained
gain and its ceiling is set by the corpus rather than by the arm.

**This is M-18's mechanism recurring in M-18's own replacement, with the sign
flipped.** `log|a|` is non-discriminating because its target is constant at `0`;
`sign(a)` off a linear gate is non-discriminating because the predictor is
saturated at `≈ 1` before training. Both are diagnostics registered against a
quantity without checking that quantity's distribution in the corpus it will be
read on. The corrected diagnostic is better than the registered one — it is at
least defined — but it is **not the discriminating instrument the kill clause
needs**, and this node reports that rather than banking `p = 0.918` as a
finding.

**The instrument that does discriminate is the gate `R²`.** It carries a real
trained gain (`0.699299` against `0.371839`, `+0.327460`) and it separates the
two populations cleanly:

  BED-M supplies `a ∈ {−1, 0, +1}`, where the oracle setting evaluates to `nan`,
  `−inf` and `inf` (§1). The bind is an expressibility statement about a gate
  range this cell never trains on.
- **`1/(1 − â_max)` is undefined at every seed**, so the amendment's predicted
  failure-mode column could not be evaluated as specified. What was measured
  instead — the gate's excursion above `1` — is the same degeneration seen in a
